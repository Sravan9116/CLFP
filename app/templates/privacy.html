CampusConnect Privacy Policy

Effective Date: University Academic Year 2026

1. Collection of Belonging Information

CampusConnect collects details submitted in lost and found reports (descriptions, locations, dates, photos). Confidential identifying characteristics entered by users are strictly quarantined from public catalog views to prevent opportunistic fraud.

2. Institutional Email Protection

Your institutional email and student/staff ID are never exposed publicly on catalog items. Direct communication occurs securely between claimants and campus administrators through the portal.

3. Data Retention and Audit Logging

Handover and recovery records are retained for university accountability and audit purposes. Disciplinary actions may be initiated if falsified evidence or identity spoofing is detected.
