Simple & Transparent Process

How CampusConnect Works

Four verified steps to protect personal belongings and reunite owners with zero fraud.

01
Report an Item

Submit a detailed report with category, approximate location, date, and description. Upload photos with secure processing.

02
Discover Matches

Our Python engine evaluates multi-attribute compatibility across text TF-IDF, colors, dates, and campus proximity.

03
Verify Ownership

Submit private ownership answers, serial numbers, or purchase evidence. Campus administrators evaluate proof securely.

04
Recover Belonging

Complete an in-person physical ID handover at designated campus security desks and receive an official PDF receipt.
