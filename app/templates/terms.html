University Property Administration Regulations

1. Truthful Reporting

All users must provide accurate and truthful details when reporting lost or found belongings. Submitting false reports or claiming items not owned by you constitutes a violation of university honor code.

2. Physical Custody & Unclaimed Items

Found belongings should be deposited at designated campus security desks or authorized collection points within 48 hours. Items unclaimed after 90 days are handled under campus abandoned property procedures.

3. In-Person Handover Verification

Claimants must present an official institutional photo ID card during scheduled handovers. Administrators reserve the right to decline handover if ownership cannot be verified beyond reasonable doubt.
