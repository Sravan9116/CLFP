How CampusConnect Operates

A secure, transparent, and intelligent platform designed to protect campus property and reunite owners with lost belongings.

01
Report Belonging

File a lost or found report specifying the item category, campus building, colors, and timeframe. Upload photos (automatically compressed) and record confidential identifying details.

02
Smart Matching

Our backend engine runs TF-IDF vector similarity on item names and descriptions, color cross-matching, campus proximity, and date compatibility to calculate a 0–100% matching confidence score.

03
Verification

Claimants submit ownership statements and proof (invoices, lockscreen descriptions, serials). Campus administrators evaluate evidence against confidential finder notes to prevent fraud.

04
Recovery

An in-person handover is scheduled at security or administrative desks. Both parties verify physical IDs, confirm recovery, and receive a formal PDF receipt with a verification QR code.
